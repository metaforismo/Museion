## Why the canvas is different

The lesson canvas is where Museion's claims are enforced: deterministic answer checks,
misconception surfacing, Maia's leak gates, and the locked transfer that removes Maia and
hints by construction. Hundreds of tests guard it. The energy language enters as
**presentation only** — the engine's truth model, session flow, and gating logic are
untouchable.

## The three moves (from the energy pass's final review)

### 1. Press grammar inside the canvas

- The Check/submit control becomes the subject-accent `Pressable`. While checking, it is
  disabled with its edge collapsed — pressed is what it is.
- **Checked-correct**: the control (or its result surface) blooms full `--color-correct`
  with the existing verified-bloom scale physics. Correct-green remains reserved for
  engine-checked outcomes — nothing else in the canvas may use it.
- **Checked-wrong**: the existing shake, plus the pressable edge collapsing — the device
  itself deflates. `--color-wrong` semantics unchanged.
- Interactive blocks (labs, ordering, diagrams) adopt press states on their controls.
  Their *evaluation* logic is untouched.

### 2. The completion loop

- Lesson completion gets a beat: the CompletionScreen leads with what the engine actually
  observed (evidence lines already exist), Maia `celebrating` animated — this is one of
  her live placements — and a full-accent Pressable back to the course.
- Returning to the course passes `?completed=<lessonId>`; `CourseTrail` reads it once and
  plays the node filling + rail segment drawing in (reduced-motion: instant fill). The
  param must be validated against the real record — a hand-typed URL for an uncompleted
  lesson animates nothing.

### 3. The board

- Trail nodes grow (~64px current, ~52px others), the rail thickens to 8–10px, and done /
  ahead rows drop to title-only; the current node keeps its full card. The path should
  read as ground at a glance.
- The duplicate tab stop at the current node goes: the circle becomes presentational and
  the Start/Continue Pressable is the single interactive element for that lesson.

## Untouchable — enforced by review

- Engine modules (`src/lib/engine/**`), session semantics, evidence recording, answer
  specs, leak gates: no edits. The canvas round changes `.tsx` presentation only.
- **The locked transfer stays stripped.** No Maia, no hints, no press-grammar celebration
  during the attempt; the energy language may not make the transfer feel assisted. Its
  verdict feedback follows the same checked-correct/checked-wrong rules afterwards.
- MaiaPanel: restyle-only (type scale, spacing, press states on its composer). Transcript
  logic, gating, provenance display logic untouched.
- Copy: no overclaim. "Correct" is a checked observation; completion copy keeps the
  observation framing.
